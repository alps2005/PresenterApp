The fifth beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. This one fixes cramped layouts reported on a 2019 iMac: the slide editor's right-hand inspector lost its margins on macOS 14 and 15.

## Install

1. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.5.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
2. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**Upgrading from beta 3 or 4:** your project opens as it is; the file format hasn't changed. From beta 2 or earlier, read the one-way upgrade note in the beta 3 release first.

## What's new

### Look
- **The editor's inspector keeps its margins.** With an image or video background, the Background controls were wider than the inspector, so every row ran past its edge. In a narrow inspector, Scaling, Dim, Loop and Mute now wrap onto their own lines, and the Background choice becomes a menu when its five buttons don't fit.
- **The font menu has a fixed width,** so a long font name on your Mac can't widen the inspector.
- **Solid inspector background,** like the rest of the editor.
- **Text boxes have inner margins:** Presenter Notes and Quick Edit's text no longer touch the edge of their box.
- **Even margins:** Quick Edit and the media bin now have the same margins as the side panels.
- **Sharper outline on non-Retina displays:** the thumbnail-size slider's outline no longer fades to a faint grey.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **Not in this beta:** themes, scripture, build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts. These are planned for 0.2.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
