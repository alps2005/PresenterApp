The fifth beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. It brings **themes**, like ProPresenter's: one look for a whole song or service that you change in one place. Text can also have an outline and a box behind it. And it fixes cramped layouts reported on a 2019 iMac, where the slide editor's right-hand inspector lost its margins.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.5.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**One-way upgrade:** this beta saves projects in a newer format (version 3). If you go back to beta 4 or earlier, it won't open them: it moves the file aside, without overwriting it, and opens a sample project instead. Keep the export from step 1 if you might go back. Your slides look the same as before until you choose a theme.

## What's new

### Themes
- **One look, many slides:** a theme sets the font, size, colour, position and effects for each kind of text (title, body and caption) and a background. Change the theme, and every slide that follows it changes.
- **Three to start with:** **Worship**; **Scripture**, with the verses top left and the reference small at the bottom; and **Lower Third**, with the words in a dark band along the bottom and no background, for a stream or camera feed.
- **Choosing a theme:** in the slide editor, **Document › Theme**. If the slides are already formatted, Presenter asks whether to keep that or use the theme for all the text. **New Song** has a theme picker too.
- **Editing a theme:** double-click it in the sidebar, under **Themes**. The editor has a live preview, and changes show on the slides as you make them. Right-click a theme to duplicate it, delete it, or **Use for New Songs**.
- **Your own touches stay:** text you style or move by hand keeps its look when the theme changes. Select it, and the Format tab says whether it follows the theme, with **Revert to Theme** and **Update Theme from This Text**.
- **Backgrounds:** a slide's own background comes first, then the presentation's, then the theme's.
- **Safe to delete:** deleting a theme leaves its presentations looking exactly the same.

### Text effects
- **Outline:** a coloured outline around each letter, so words stay readable over busy backgrounds.
- **Box:** a filled, rounded box behind the words, with adjustable space around them.
- They're in the editor's Format tab, in themes, and in Quick Edit under **Effects…**.

### Look
- **The editor's inspector keeps its margins.** With an image or video background, the Background controls were wider than the inspector, so every row ran past its edge. In a narrow inspector, Scaling, Dim, Loop and Mute now wrap onto their own lines, and the Background choice becomes a menu when its five buttons don't fit.
- **The live panel keeps its margins too:** in a narrow window, the Timers, Messages, Props and Stage choice becomes a menu when its four buttons don't fit.
- **The font menu has a fixed width,** so a long font name on your Mac can't widen the inspector.
- **Solid inspector background,** like the rest of the editor.
- **Text boxes are easier to see:** Presenter Notes and Quick Edit's text box have an outline (the notes box used to disappear into the dark inspector), and their text no longer touches the edge.
- **Even margins:** Quick Edit and the media bin now have the same margins as the side panels.
- **Sharper outline on non-Retina displays:** the thumbnail-size slider's outline no longer fades to a faint grey.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **Not in this beta:** scripture, build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts. These are planned for 0.2.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
