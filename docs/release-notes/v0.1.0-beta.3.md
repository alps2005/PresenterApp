The third beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. This one is about trust: your project is safer, and the operator window is easier to read in a dark booth.

## Install

1. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.3.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**. If you used an earlier beta, export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**One-way upgrade:** this beta saves projects in a newer format (version 2). If you go back to beta 2, it won't open them: it moves the file aside, without overwriting it, and opens a sample project instead. Keep the export from step 1 if you might go back.

## What's new

### Your project is safer
- **Undo keeps your media:** undoing an edit no longer removes an image or video you imported after it.
- **Automatic backups:** each time Presenter opens your project, it saves a copy in `Backups/` and keeps the newest 10.
- **Quitting can't silently lose changes:** if saving fails when you quit, Presenter tells you why and lets you cancel.
- **Large projects don't freeze the window:** Export Project… and Open Project… run in the background with a progress sheet, even with videos inside.

### Easier to read during a service
- **Dark by default**, with solid panels instead of see-through glass over your slides. To follow your Mac's light or dark setting instead, turn on View › Follow System Appearance.
- **Clearer status badges:** each one has an icon and a word, in a larger size.
  - BLACKOUT, BACKGROUND ONLY, CLEARED and NOTHING LIVE each look different.
  - CUED is always blue.
- **Live and cued slides are outlined the same way everywhere.** That includes Up Next, Cued, and the Media Bin, which now shows LIVE on the item that's on screen.
- **Blackout** is set apart from Clear and Restore, so it's harder to hit by mistake.
- **Toolbar:** on macOS 26 and later, the Quick Edit, Media and Output buttons stand on their own, and the ones that are on show clearly.
- **Warnings show in amber**, and timer values are larger.
- **Wording:** "Take Live" is used everywhere.
- **Deleting a timer or a message asks first**, and you can undo it.
- **Accessibility:** VoiceOver reads the icon-only buttons, and scrolling respects Reduce Motion.

### Show
- **Video mute sticks:** muting a video from the transport now lasts while the same video keeps playing under later slides.

## Not yet tested on a second display

Beta 3 rewrites how Presenter decides which screen each output goes to, and how video mute is tracked. Both are covered by automated tests, but haven't been re-checked on a real projector since beta 2. Please report anything odd with:
- the stage display full screen on its own display;
- unplugging and replugging a display during a service: the output should come back full screen on the right screen;
- video mute lasting across slide changes on the projector.

These checks are planned for beta 4.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **Not in this beta:** build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them.
