The ninth beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. Slides can now **move on by themselves** (a Slideshow for announcement loops, or a timer on any slide), there are **four new slide transitions**, and text, shapes and images can have **their own transitions**.

## Install

1. **Export your project first** (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S). This beta saves projects in a new format that earlier betas can't open, so the export is your way back.
2. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.9.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**File format 4:** your project is upgraded the first time beta 9 saves it. Betas 8 and earlier can't open it after that; they move it aside instead of overwriting it.

## What's new

### Slides that move on by themselves
- **Slideshow:** click **Slideshow** in a presentation's header and choose 5, 8, 10, 15 or 30 seconds, or **Custom…**. Every slide moves on after that time, and after the last it starts again from the first. Good for announcement loops before the service.
- **Go To Next Timer:** right-click a slide and choose **Go To Next Timer…** (or use **Go To Next** in the slide editor) to give one slide its own time. It comes before the Slideshow's. **Loop to the first slide** goes back to the start; without it, the last slide moves on to the next item in the show, as → does.
- **Videos:** a slide whose video background plays once (Loop off) waits for the video to end before its countdown starts, so 0 seconds moves on the moment it finishes. A looping video doesn't hold the slide up.
- **Seeing it:** slides that move on by themselves show their time under the thumbnail, and the live one fills a small red circle as it counts down. The Live panel shows **Next slide in…** with a **Stop** button (Live › Stop Auto-Advance, ⌥⌘.).
- **Staying in control:** pressing → or ←, or taking another slide live, starts that slide's own countdown. Clear and Blackout stop it, and Restore starts it again.

### Transitions
- **New slide transitions:** in the slide editor's **Animate** tab, next to Dissolve: **Fade Through Black**, **Push**, **Wipe** and **Zoom**. Push and Wipe come in from the left, right, top or bottom.
- **Element transitions:** select a text, shape or image in the slide editor and give it its own **In** and **Out** transition (Fade, Push, Wipe or Zoom), for when the whole slide shouldn't move. They play when the slide changes: one → brings the slide, its elements transition in (an In can wait a few seconds), and the previous slide's elements transition out without holding the new slide back. Slides with element transitions show ✦ under their thumbnail.
- **See them in the Live panel:** the Audience preview now plays transitions exactly as the audience sees them.
- **Smoother Dissolve:** the old slide's words fade out as the new ones fade in, instead of staying at full strength until the end.

## Known limitations

- **One-way format:** see Install. Keep your export from beta 8 if you might go back.
- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show. We're working on it.
- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **The stage display** shows the live slide's words straight away; it doesn't play element transitions.
- **Not in this beta:** scripture, audio-only tracks, several shows, library folders and custom stage display layouts. These are planned.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
