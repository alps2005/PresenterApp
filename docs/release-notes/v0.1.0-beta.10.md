The tenth beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. It adds **Scripture**: type a reference in English or Spanish, or search for words, and add the passage to the show as slides. Themes get a few fixes too.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.10.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Projects keep the same format as beta 9 (version 4), so you can switch between beta 9 and beta 10 freely. Coming from beta 8 or earlier, read the beta 9 notes first: that upgrade is one-way.

## What's new

### Scripture
- **Add Scripture… (⌥⌘V, or Scripture… in the sidebar):** type a reference like *Juan 3:16-18*, *1 Co 13:4-7*, *Jn 3:16*, *Salmo 23* or *Mateo 5:3-12; 6:9-13*. Book names and abbreviations work in English and Spanish, and accents and capitals don't matter. Or search for words and click a verse.
- **Check before adding:** the sheet shows the slides the passage will make. Choose 1 to 4 verses per slide, small verse numbers, and the reference on each slide (like *Juan 3:16-17 · RVR1909*). A long verse is split at a sentence or clause.
- **Ready to read:** new passages follow the **Scripture** theme, with the verses at the top left and the reference small at the bottom. They're ordinary slides, so you can edit them like any other.
- **Your own Bibles:** Presenter doesn't include or download any. **Import Bible…** reads Zefania or OSIS XML files, the free formats public-domain translations are shared in. The README says where to find them. Use only public-domain or freely licensed translations.

### Themes
- **Copies stay where you can see them:** duplicating (⌘D) or pasting text that a theme places, like Scripture's verses, puts the copy beside the original instead of exactly on top of it.
- **Revert to Theme in Quick Edit:** on the Style tab, when the cued slide's text has its own formatting.
- **A fuller theme preview:** the theme editor shows the title, body and caption together, with each one on its own below. Click one to edit it.

## Known limitations

- **Bible formats:** Zefania and OSIS XML only; USFX and other formats aren't read yet.
- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show.
- **Not in this beta:** audio-only tracks, several shows, library folders and custom stage display layouts. These are planned.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
