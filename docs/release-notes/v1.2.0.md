Presenter 1.2 **updates itself** from now on, opens with a **welcome window** to pick your show, **imports songs** from OpenLyrics, ChordPro and SongSelect files, runs **slide actions and macros**, and adds a **second output** with its own look, for a livestream or a lobby screen.

## Install

**This is the last version you download by hand.** From here on, Presenter checks for updates once a day and installs them when you agree.

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v1.2.0.dmg** below and open it. Drag **Presenter** onto the **Applications** shortcut, replacing the older version, then eject the disk image. Open Presenter from Applications, not from the disk image: a copy opened from there can't update itself.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**This upgrade is one-way.** 1.2 saves projects in a new format (version 7). Once 1.2 opens your project, Presenter 1.1 can't open it, so keep the export from step 1 if you might go back. Everything carries over.

## What's new

### Updates
- **Presenter updates itself:** it checks once a day (it asks the first time whether it may) or when you choose **Presenter › Check for Updates…**. It shows what's new and asks before installing, then restarts as the new version, with no new security prompt.
- **Never during a service:** while anything is live or an output is on, it doesn't pop up. A quiet **Update to…** item appears in the Presenter menu and the welcome window instead, and an update you accept waits until nothing is live.
- **Signed and checked:** Presenter only installs an update signed with the project's key, so a tampered download is refused. **Settings…** (⌘,) turns the daily check off.

### Welcome window
- **Pick your show first:** Presenter opens with a window like Xcode's welcome window. It shows your shows, most recently opened first, with **Open Project…**, **Import Songs…** and **New Show** (its arrow also duplicates the selected show). Double-click a show, or select it and press Return, to start presenting.
- **Outputs wait for you:** nothing reaches the screens until a show is open.
- **Closing it quits Presenter** at launch. Window › **Welcome to Presenter** (⇧⌘1) opens it again later; then closing it just closes it, and during a service it never quits. Untick **Show this window when Presenter opens** to start in the operator window instead. **File › Shows…** (⇧⌘O) still switches shows without leaving the operator window.

### Second output and looks
- **A second audience output** for a livestream or a lobby TV: Output › **Second Output**, or the toolbar's Output menu, in a window or full screen.
- **A look for each audience output** (toolbar Output menu › **Look…**, with a live preview):
  - Leave out the background, the slide, props or messages.
  - Without the background, show black or a **key colour**, such as green, so a video mixer can put the words over a camera.
  - Restyle the words with another theme, for example a lower third for the stream. Words formatted by hand keep their look.
- Looks are saved on this Mac, like the output screens. Blackout blacks out every output.

### Songs
- **Import Songs** (File › Import Songs…, ⇧⌘I): OpenLyrics, ChordPro, SongSelect (lyrics .txt and SongPro .usr) and plain-text lyrics, several files at once. Chords are left out, sections become labelled groups, and a file's singing order becomes a **Song Order** arrangement.
- **Song details:** writers, copyright and CCLI song number are kept from imported files and can be edited in the slide editor under **Document › Song**.

### Show control
- **Slide actions:** a slide can start, pause, reset or restart timers, show a message or prop, change the stage message or layout, stop audio and run a macro when it's taken live (slide editor › Format › Actions). A ⚡ on the thumbnail marks slides that have actions.
- **Macros:** buttons in the Live panel's new **Macros** tab that run a named list of actions at one click, like "Pre-Service" (logo, countdown, walk-in music) or "End" (clear everything). Right-click a macro to edit it.

### Scripture
- **USFX and USX Bibles:** Import Bible… also reads USFX files and USX Bibles (choose the folder or its book files). A Digital Bible Library `metadata.xml`, in the folder or beside a single file, names the translation.
- **Rename…** in the Scripture sheet changes a Bible's name and the abbreviation slides show.

### Fixes
- **Take Live** is grey while nothing is cued; it used to stay bright red.
- An output whose remembered screen has the operator window says so, instead of "disconnected", and fills that screen as soon as the operator window moves to another one.
- In the stage layout editor, a region's name shows only while the pointer is over it, so names no longer cover overlapping regions.
- The show that's open at launch now counts as opened, so deleting another show goes back to it.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install). Updates don't ask again.
- **Bible formats:** Zefania, OSIS, USFX and USX.
- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show.
- **Live values in slides** update on the outputs and the stage display; thumbnails show the words in braces.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
