Presenter 1.3 shows at a glance what each item in the sidebar is: a **presentation**, a **song** or a **Bible passage**.

**This upgrade is one-way.** 1.3 saves projects in a new format (version 8). Once 1.3 opens your project, Presenter 1.2 can't open it. If you might go back, export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S). Everything carries over.

## Install

If you have Presenter 1.2, choose **Presenter › Check for Updates…**, or wait for the daily check, and install it from there.

For a new install, download **Presenter-v1.3.0.dmg** below, open it, drag **Presenter** onto the **Applications** shortcut and eject the disk image. The first time, macOS blocks the app because it isn't notarized: double-click it, close the warning, then click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

## Sidebar

- **An icon for each kind of item:** in the show and the library, presentations have a play-screen icon, songs have music notes and Bible passages have a book, matching the **New Presentation**, **New Song…** and **Scripture…** buttons.
- **Existing items are recognised:** songs with credits, section names (Verse, Chorus…) or a song order show as songs, and passages added with Scripture… show as scripture. Everything else shows as a presentation.
- **Show As:** right-click an item and choose **Show As › Presentation, Song or Scripture** to change it, for example a song pasted without section names.
