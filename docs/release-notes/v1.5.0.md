Presenter 1.5 makes it quick to show a verse the pastor calls out: **Scripture over the cued slide** puts the passage on top of any slide, live, without adding a presentation to the show. This release also fixes the CCLI report's CSV in Excel.

Projects keep the same file format as 1.4, so Presenter 1.4 can still open them.

## Install

If you have Presenter 1.2 or later, choose **Presenter › Check for Updates…**, or wait for the daily check, and install it from there.

For a new install, download **Presenter-v1.5.0.dmg** below, open it, drag **Presenter** onto the **Applications** shortcut and eject the disk image. The first time, macOS blocks the app because it isn't notarized: double-click it, close the warning, then click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

## Scripture

- **Scripture over the cued slide:** cue any slide (the sermon title, say), then click **Scripture…** under the Cued slide in the Live panel, or choose **Live › Scripture Over Cued Slide…** (⌥⌘L). Type a reference or search, and click **Show Live**: the slide goes live with the verses on top, in the theme you choose there (**Scripture** to start). Nothing is added to the library or the show.
- The window warns that the verses show **on top of the cued slide**, whose words and pictures stay underneath, and previews each page over it, so you can see any overlap before it's on screen.
- **Next** and **Previous** step through the verses; after the last one, Next carries on with the slide after the cued one. **Remove Verses** in the Live panel leaves the slide on its own. Clear Slide and Restore keep your place in the passage.

## Fixes

- **CCLI Report CSV in Excel:** the exported file now opens in Excel on a Mac with accents intact (Océanos), not garbled. Numbers and Google Sheets read it as before.
