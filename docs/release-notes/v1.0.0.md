Presenter 1.0, the first stable release of the free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. After ten betas it adds **Shows**, so one project can hold your Sunday service, midweek meeting and Easter service side by side, **library folders**, and **audio**: music and sounds you can play from a bin, from a slide or as a presentation's soundtrack.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v1.0.0.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**This upgrade is one-way.** 1.0 saves projects in a new format (version 5). Once 1.0 opens your project, the betas can't open it, so keep the export from step 1 if you might go back. Your current playlist becomes a show, and everything else carries over as it was.

## What's new

### Shows
- **More than one show:** a project can hold several shows: a Sunday service, a midweek meeting, Easter. Each is its own running order, and they all share one library, so fixing a typo in a song fixes it in every show that has it.
- **The Shows window** opens at launch and from **File › Shows…** (⇧⌘O). It lists your shows, most recently opened first. Open, make, rename, duplicate or delete one there. Untick **Show this window when Presenter opens** to skip it at launch.
- **Switching shows is safe during a service:** what's on the audience screen stays there until you take a slide live from the new show.
- **Deleting a show** keeps its presentations in the library. Undo brings it back, unless it was the show you had open.

### Library folders
- **Folders** keep the library tidy: songs in one, announcements in another. Click **New Folder…** in the Library, then drag presentations onto it, or right-click one and choose **Move To**.
- **Drag a presentation onto the Library header** to take it out of its folder. Deleting a folder keeps its presentations. Every change can be undone.

### Audio
- **Audio bin** (⌥⌘U, or the **Audio** button in the toolbar): import music and sounds (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF and more), then click one to play it for the room. Right-click to play it on a loop.
- **Slide audio:** a slide can start a track, with an optional fade-in and loop, or fade out whatever is playing, when it's taken live. Set it in the slide editor, or right-click a track in the bin and choose **Play When the Cued Slide Goes Live**. Slides with audio show a small note (or a muted speaker) under their thumbnail. Taking the same slide live again never restarts the music.
- **Soundtracks:** a presentation can have its own background music, which starts when you move into it from another item, such as a looping pad under the announcements.
- **In the Live panel** you see the playing track, with pause, a position slider, the time left and Stop, which fades it out. Changing tracks crossfades. **Clear All** fades the audio out; **Blackout** leaves it playing. **Live › Stop Audio** and **Pause or Play Audio** work from anywhere.
- **Export Project…** includes your audio files, so a project moved to another Mac plays its music there too.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Bible formats:** Zefania and OSIS XML only; USFX and other formats aren't read yet.
- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show.
- **Not yet:** custom stage display layouts, remembered displays and live timers inside slides. These are planned next.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
