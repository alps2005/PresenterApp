Presenter 1.4 brings ProPresenter's habits to the booth: **section keys** to jump to a verse or chorus, **CCLI credits** on screen and a **usage report**, a passage in **two Bibles** at once, a **pre-service check**, a chosen **sound output**, and imports of **PDFs** and **ProPresenter songs**.

**This upgrade is one-way.** 1.4 saves projects in a new format (version 9). Once 1.4 opens your project, Presenter 1.3 can't open it. If you might go back, export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S). Everything carries over.

## Install

If you have Presenter 1.2 or 1.3, choose **Presenter › Check for Updates…**, or wait for the daily check, and install it from there.

For a new install, download **Presenter-v1.4.0.dmg** below, open it, drag **Presenter** onto the **Applications** shortcut and eject the disk image. The first time, macOS blocks the app because it isn't notarized: double-click it, close the warning, then click **Open Anyway** in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

## Live

- **Section keys:** with the show focused, a letter takes a section of the live song live, in its song order: **V** verse, **C** chorus, **P** pre-chorus, **B** bridge, **T** tag, **I** intro, **E** ending, **N** interlude. Press a number after the letter for one in particular (**V** then **2** = Verse 2), or the letter again to move on to the next one. English and Spanish section names both work (Verso, Coro, Puente…).

## Songs and CCLI

- **Credits on screen:** a song with credits shows a small line with its title, writers, copyright, CCLI song number and your church's licence number on its first and last slides, as CCLI asks. Each output's **Look…** can leave it out, for a stream with its own captions.
- **CCLI Report** (File › CCLI Report…): enter your church's CCLI licence number, turn the credits on or off, and see which songs were shown between two dates and how many times. **Export CSV…** saves it for CCLI's online reporting. A song counts once a day, when it goes live; the log stays on this Mac. Counting starts with this version.
- **ProPresenter songs:** **Import Songs…** reads ProPresenter 7 files (.pro): each song's words, groups, selected arrangement and CCLI details come across. Formatting, media and translations stay behind.
- **SongSelect files** fill in your licence number if it's still empty.

## Scripture

- **Two Bibles at once:** in **Add Scripture…**, choose a **Second Bible** to show the passage in another translation below the first, the same verses on each slide, for bilingual services. The sheet says when the second Bible doesn't have the passage.

## Show

- **Pre-Service Check** (Live › Pre-Service Check…, ⌥⌘K): a checklist for the open show, with **Show** to go to each item: missing media files, fonts not installed on this Mac, an audience output that's off, disconnected or behind the operator window, presentations with nothing on their slides, and songs without credits. At launch it opens by itself only when something needs attention.

## Audio

- **Choose the sound output** (Presenter › Settings… › Audio): music, audio cues, soundtracks and video sound play through the device you pick, such as the interface feeding the sound desk, so a Bluetooth headset or a change to the Mac's sound settings can't send the service's sound elsewhere. If it's unplugged, sound falls back to the Mac's output and the Live panel says so.

## Library

- **Import PDF** (File › Import PDF…): each page becomes a slide, filling the screen over black. Keynote, PowerPoint and Google Slides decks come in this way after exporting them as a PDF.
