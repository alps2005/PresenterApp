The second beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. This one adds the show tools from ProPresenter that matter most on a Sunday: a stage display for the people on stage, timers, messages, props, and video backgrounds.

## Install

1. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.2.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**. If you used beta 1, export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## What's new

- **Stage display (⌥⌘S):** a second output just for the stage, in a window or full screen on its own display. It shows the live slide's words in large type, the next slide, a clock, a timer, the slide's notes, and a message banner ("5 minutes left"). The audience never sees it.
- **Timers:** countdowns (e.g. a 30-minute sermon), counting down to a time of day (service at 10:00), or counting up. Start, pause and reset them from **Show Controls** in the Live panel.
- **Messages:** a bar over the slides such as "Parent of child {Number}, please come to the nursery". Fill in the blanks when you show it, or add `{timer:Sermon}` for a live countdown. Clear Messages is ⌥⌘G.
- **Props:** graphics that stay up across slides, like a logo, made in the slide editor. Several can be on at once. Clear Props is ⌥⌘P.
- **Video backgrounds and a Media Bin (⌥⌘I):** import videos and images, then click one to put it behind the words. Videos loop and keep playing as the slides change, with play/pause, scrubbing, loop, mute and volume in the Live panel.
- **Layers and transparent backgrounds:** a slide with no background leaves the image or video already on screen, like ProPresenter. Clear Slide (⌥⌘C), Clear Background (⌥⌘X) and Clear All (⌥⌘A) clear each layer, and Restore brings them back.
- **Song tools:**
  - **Reflow:** edit a whole song as text and watch the slides update.
  - **Arrangements:** play the groups in any order with repeats (V1 C V2 C B C C).
  - **Library and search:** every presentation, including ones taken off the playlist, searchable by title and words.
  - **Thumbnail size slider.**
- **Second display tested on a real projector:** full screen, Blackout and Restore, and unplugging and replugging the cable all work.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **Not in this beta:** build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them.
