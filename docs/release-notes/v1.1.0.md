Presenter 1.1 is about the stage and the screens. Design what the people on stage see with **stage layouts**, show a running **timer or the clock inside a slide**, and let Presenter **remember which screen** each output goes to.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v1.1.0.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

**This upgrade is one-way.** 1.1 saves projects in a new format (version 6). Once 1.1 opens your project, Presenter 1.0 can't open it, so keep the export from step 1 if you might go back. Everything carries over, and the stage display keeps the look it had.

## What's new

### Stage layouts
- **Choose what the stage shows:** in the Live panel's **Stage** tab, pick a layout. Projects start with three:
  - **Lyrics:** big words and what's coming next, for singers and the band.
  - **Speaker:** the clock, a large timer and the slide's notes, for the preacher.
  - **Slides:** the live and next slides as pictures, with the clock and timer.
  - **Standard** keeps the stage display as it was in 1.0.
- **Make your own:** **Edit…** opens the layout editor. Add regions (the live or next words, the live or next slide, the clock, a timer, the notes, the stage message, or your own text) and drag and resize them on a live preview. They snap to edges and centres; hold ⌘ to place one freely. Each region has its own text size, colour and alignment.
- **Changes show straight away** on the stage display, and every change can be undone with ⌘Z.

### Live timers and the clock in slides
- **Type `{timer:Sermon}`** in a slide's words and the audience sees the Sermon timer counting, like *"Service starts in 4:59"*. **`{clock}`** shows the time, and **`{clock:seconds}`** the time with seconds.
- **Insert them from the slide editor:** select the text and use **Live Values › Insert**. If a timer name doesn't match one of your timers, the editor says so.
- **Lyrics are safe:** other words in braces stay as you typed them. The slide thumbnails and the editor show what you typed; the audience output, the Live panel preview and the stage display show the running time.

### Outputs remember their screens
- **Set it up once:** Presenter remembers, on this Mac, whether the audience output and the stage display were on, and which screen each one was full screen on. At the next launch they come back where they were.
- **Found even if macOS renames them:** displays are recognised by their serial number (or make, model and name), so a projector that reconnects with a new ID is still found. If it isn't connected yet when Presenter starts, the output waits and appears when it's plugged in.

### Fixes
- Message blanks typed with spaces inside the braces, like `{ Number }`, are now filled in.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Bible formats:** Zefania and OSIS XML only; USFX and other formats aren't read yet.
- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show.
- **Live values in slides** update on the audience output and stage display; thumbnails show the words in braces.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
