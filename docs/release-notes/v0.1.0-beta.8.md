The eighth beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. It fixes **clicking songs in the Library** on some Macs, keeps the sidebar's buttons readable with a long library, and does **less work while you scroll the show**.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.8.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Projects keep the same format as betas 5 to 7 (version 3), so you can switch between them freely.

## What's new

### Library
- **Click anywhere on a row:** on some Macs, a song or presentation in the Library only responded to a click on the edge of its row, not on its name. Now the whole row works.
- **Double-click or Return** adds the selected Library item to the show.
- **Readable buttons:** with a long library, its rows no longer show through the New Presentation and New Song buttons at the bottom of the sidebar.

### Performance
- **Smoother scrolling with outputs open:** as you scroll the show, the presentation at the top is selected. That no longer redraws the audience output, the stage display and the menus each time.

## Known limitations

- **Scrolling on older Intel Macs** can still feel heavy with a long show. We're measuring it and working on it.
- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **One show at a time:** a project has one show. Several shows and library folders are planned.
- **Not in this beta:** automatic slide advancing, scripture, build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts. These are planned.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
