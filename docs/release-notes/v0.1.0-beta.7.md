The seventh beta of Presenter, a free macOS app for running slides at church services and live events. Clicking a song or presentation in the **Library** now shows its slides instead of an error, and you can **rename the show**.

## Install

1. Export your project first (File › Export Project…, ⇧⌘S) as a backup.
2. Download **Presenter-v0.1.0-beta.7.zip** below, unzip it, and drag **Presenter** into **Applications**, replacing the older version.
3. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Double-click it, close the warning, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the Presenter message. After that it opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Projects keep the same format as betas 5 and 6 (version 3), so you can switch between them freely.

## What's new

### Library
- **Look through a Library item:** click a song or presentation in the Library to see its slides in the workspace. Before, this showed "That playlist item no longer exists."
- **Edit it or add it from there:** **Edit** (or double-clicking a slide) opens it in the slide editor, and **Add to Show** adds it to the show and takes you back to it. **Back to Show** returns without adding it. Library slides can't go live until they're in the show.
- **Themes:** clicking a theme no longer shows that error either. Double-click a theme to edit it.

### Show
- **Rename the show:** right-click the show's name at the top of the sidebar and choose **Rename Show…**. The window title shows the new name, and ⌘Z undoes it.

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized:** macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time it opens (see Install).
- **Stage display choice:** after the app restarts, the stage display opens in a window; choose its screen again from the Output menu.
- **Export file size:** exported projects include their images and videos, so they can be large.
- **One show at a time:** a project has one show. Several shows and library folders are planned.
- **Not in this beta:** scripture, build animations, audio-only tracks, and custom stage display layouts. These are planned for 0.2.

Please report problems on the repository's Issues page, with the steps that led to them and your macOS version. Security problems can be reported privately from the **Security** tab.
